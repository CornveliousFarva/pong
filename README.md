# pong
Paddle-based Pong game with multiple levels, paddle colors, and balls.

Oh, pong, the origin of the video game. Long before story-driven video games that take months to complete and years to develop wuth acheivements designed to be a constant dopamine hit, there was two paddles and a bouncing pixel across a screen. It's almost hypnotic to watch it float across the screen.

That said, this is where it will get more complicated: this will have levels modeled after the Grand Slam of tennis along with the classic black & white and also allow the user to select their own color of paddles and balls. That will be the MVP.

For planned features, here is what I plan on doing:
- Tournament progression mode
- Sound effects and music
- Enhanced AI difficulty scaling
- Animated UI transitions
- Save system and statistics
